OkToChat features

Last updated 2026-11-24

Today

The home screen. It greets you and shows what is new in the feed, the latest chat messages, your next tasks, recent notes and drawings, and how long you focused today. Quick buttons let you write a note, add a task, open a chat or ask OkAI in one tap.

Chats

  • Private chats and groups. Private messages, including photos, voice messages, replies and reactions, are encrypted on the phone before they are sent, so the server only stores scrambled text.
  • Global chat with channels, where messages disappear after 3 days.
  • GIFs, photos, replies and reactions.
  • Voice messages, polls and hangouts (plan a meetup in a chat; OkAI posts a reminder before it starts).
  • What you missed: a short summary of a busy chat.
  • Several accounts on one phone.

Calls

Voice and video calls, one to one or in groups of up to 8 people.

The feed

Posts with photos and short videos, comments and likes. Images go through an on-device filter, and content that is reported by three people is hidden until it is reviewed.

OkAI

OkAI is the built-in AI assistant. Type @ok in any chat, group, comment or post to ask it something, or use the separate Ask page for questions, rewrites, ideas and explanations (it can also use your notes, if you choose). Before answering factual questions, OkAI can look the topic up on Wikipedia. It can also draw pictures ("@ok draw a cat in space"): 3 pictures a day for free, more with Plus. Every picture request is checked for safety first.

Music

  • Music player for your own songs, saved on your device, with favorites, playlists, playback speed and a sleep timer.
  • Discover and Listen together: play a song from Audius and friends follow along in real time.
  • Beat maker with tempo, swing and a playable keyboard (tap the keys, or use a computer keyboard). Beats can be exported or posted.

Drawing studio

A full drawing app with brushes, smudge, fill, eraser, lasso and move tools, and canvas sizes for squares, portraits, landscapes, phone wallpapers and stories. Drawings are saved in a gallery and can be shared in chats and posts.

OkToBrowse

A built-in web browser with results from DuckDuckGo, and quick links to Google, Wikipedia and YouTube. You can save your favorite sites.

Tools

  • Notes and tasks (tasks can be grouped).
  • Focus: a focus timer for work and study sessions, with your total focus time shown on Today.
  • Calculator and Convert, a unit converter.

Games

  • OkToBrawl: a fighting game built into the app, with online 1v1 matches against friends, ranked play and a story mode.
  • Chess: challenge a friend from a private chat.
  • OkToChat Story on the Nintendo DSi (see Console apps).

OkToChat Pass

Pass lets two phones that pass each other in real life swap a small profile card, like a digital business card. It is off until you turn it on, works in the Android app on Android 12 or newer, and uses Bluetooth only: it does not use your location.

OkToChat Plus

Plus is optional and paid through Google Play. It adds cosmetic extras, like profile decorations, and more OkAI pictures per day. Everything else in OkToChat stays free.

Languages and accessibility

The app is available in 16 languages, has a light and a dark theme, and can be installed from the website on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS through the browser.
