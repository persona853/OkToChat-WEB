OkToChat on Nintendo DSi, 3DS and Wii

Last updated 2026-11-24

OkToChat has small homebrew apps that bring chatting to old Nintendo consoles. They need a console that can run homebrew software. The apps are free and can be downloaded from the OkToChat app (Account page) or the OkToChat website.

Signing in

A console shows a short code. You type that code in the OkToChat app on your phone, and the console is linked to your account. The console then keeps a sign-in key on its SD card or memory. Messages sent from a console are marked with a small tag, like "from a DSi".

Nintendo DSi

The DSi app (OkToChatDS.nds) can read the feed, chat, talk to OkAI and ask OkAI for pictures, which are shown on the DSi screen. It also includes the game OkToChat Story.

Nintendo 3DS and Wii

The 3DS and Wii apps (OkToChat3DS and OkToChatWii) let you chat on OkToChat from those consoles. The Wii app runs from the Homebrew Channel.

OkToChat Story

OkToChat Story: the In-Between is a short role-playing game for the Nintendo DSi, inside the OkToChat DSi app. Kai's OkToChat message gets stuck on "Sending..." and pulls him into the In-Between, a maze of strange places where unsent messages go. It mixes pixel art with real photographs of everyday objects and has a lightly creepy, mostly funny tone.

  • 6 levels: Yellow Halls (Sending...), The Pool Rooms (Unread), After-Hours Mall (The Feed), The Office That Never Ends (Moderation), Parking Level -99 (Low Signal) and Home? (Delivered).
  • Battles where you can FIGHT, use an ITEM, LET GO of an enemy once it is calm, or TALK: you type anything you want to say, and OkAI decides how the enemy reacts. Then you dodge its attack with a small yellow OK square.
  • Characters include Mira (Kai's rival), Bolt (a tiny robot "hero"), Gus (an arcade ghost stuck since 1998), Wanda (who sells exits), The Microwave (the save point) and The Landline, a rotary phone that rings in every level.
  • 6 endings, depending on who you spare, which choices you make and whether you answer The Landline.
