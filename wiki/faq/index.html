OkToChat FAQ

Last updated 2026-11-24

Is OkToChat free?

Yes. OkToChat is free, with no ads and no subscriptions. There is an optional paid extra called OkToChat Plus, which only adds cosmetic items and more OkAI pictures. Nothing free is ever moved behind Plus.

Where can I get OkToChat?

You can use OkToChat in any web browser at oktochat.github.io, and install it from there like an app on a computer. The Android app is currently in testing on Google Play. The Nintendo DSi, 3DS and Wii apps can be downloaded from the account page of the app.

Is OkToChat safe? Are my messages private?

Private messages are encrypted on your phone before they are sent, so the server only stores scrambled text. OkToChat has no ads, does not track you and does not sell or share your data. Pass uses Bluetooth, not your location. Read the privacy policy for the details.

What is OkAI?

OkAI is the AI assistant built into OkToChat. Type @ok in a chat to ask it anything, or ask it to draw a picture. It can look facts up on Wikipedia before answering.

How old do I need to be?

OkToChat is for people aged 16 and older.

What is OkToBrawl?

OkToBrawl is a fighting game built into the OkToChat app, with online matches against friends, ranked play and a story mode.

Can I really use OkToChat on a Nintendo DSi?

Yes. OkToChat has homebrew apps for the Nintendo DSi, 3DS and Wii. You link the console to your account with a short code. The DSi app also includes a game, OkToChat Story.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from the account page in the app. The privacy policy explains exactly what is deleted.

How do I contact OkToChat?

Write to oktosupport@virgilio.it.
