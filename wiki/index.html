Overview

OkToChat started as a simple idea: one app to talk to friends, without ads and without selling anyone's data. It grew into a full chat app with encrypted private messages, group calls, a global chat with channels, a feed of posts, creative tools and its own AI assistant.

The app runs on Android and in any modern web browser, from the same code. On a computer, the website can be installed like a normal app. The project also has homebrew apps for old Nintendo consoles, so people can chat from a Nintendo DSi, 3DS or Wii.

Principles

OkToChat follows a few rules that apply to every new feature:

  • Free stays free. Nothing that is free today is ever moved behind a payment.
  • No ads and no selling or sharing of people's data, ever.
  • Plus is only extras: cosmetic items and things that cost real money to run, like extra OkAI pictures.
  • No pressure tactics: no nagging pop-ups, fake urgency or misleading claims.

Name

"OkToChat" reads as "OK to chat". The logo is a yellow square with the letters OK. The AI assistant, OkAI, is summoned by typing @ok.
