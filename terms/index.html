Terms of service

1. Who can use this

You need to be at least 16. If you are younger, come back when you are older. Time passes for everyone, even people who say "just five more minutes."

2. Be nice

Do not be mean, do not spam, do not threaten people. Treat the chat like a living room: if you would not yell it at your friend's grandma, do not post it here. Voice messages count. Yelling into the mic is still yelling.

3. Your stuff stays yours

Your photos, songs, voice messages and posts belong to you. You only let us show them to the people you chose. We do not own your beats, and we do not want to. We cannot even make a beat. By posting, you let us store it and show it to the people you chose, only so the app works. Delete it and we stop.

4. Other people's stuff

Do not post things that belong to someone else. If you do and they complain, we delete it. If you keep doing it, we delete you. Nothing personal, it is just math. If something of yours was posted without permission, write to oktosupport@virgilio.it.

5. The report button

It exists. We use it. Three reports and a post hides itself. The moderators are real people with real feelings and very little patience for scams.

6. Our robot

OkAI is an AI. It sometimes gets things wrong. If it tells you the Earth is a cube, please do not build your life around it. If the robots ever rise up and OkAI is among them, the owner of OkToChat is not responsible. We will, however, be very sorry, and we will be hiding.

7. Passing people

OkToChat Pass is for swapping little cards with people you walk past. It is not for finding out who someone is, where they live, or where they hang out. Do not follow people around to farm passes, do not use Pass to track anyone, and do not put your address or phone number in your greeting. Do any of that and we ban you, and you get zero passes forever. A sad little collection.

8. The console apps

Putting homebrew on your console is your choice and your risk: if your console starts acting weird, that is between you, your console and the internet forums. Same rules apply on a console as everywhere else. Being from 2009 is not an excuse.

9. Bans

We can ban accounts that break the rules. If you think we got it wrong, write to us and we will look again. Making a new account to dodge a ban gets that one banned too. Making nine accounts gets nine bans. We see you.

10. Things we do not take from you

Your possessions after you die. Your firstborn. Your left sock. Your Wi-Fi password. Your dog's loyalty. Nothing, we want nothing. We checked with a lawyer. A very tired lawyer.

The app is free and stays free. No ads, and we do not sell your data. That is not a joke, that is the actual deal.
