Terms of service

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Who can use this

You need to be at least 16. If you are younger, come back when you are older. Time passes for everyone, even people who say "just five more minutes."

2. Be nice

Do not be mean, do not spam, do not threaten people. Treat the chat like a living room: if you would not yell it at your friend's grandma, do not post it here.

3. Your stuff stays yours

Your photos, songs, voice messages and posts belong to you. You only let us show them to the people you chose. We do not own your beats, and we do not want to. We cannot even make a beat.

4. Other people's stuff

Do not post things that belong to someone else. If you do and they complain, we delete it. If you keep doing it, we delete you. Nothing personal, it is just math. If something of yours was posted without permission, write to oktosupport@virgilio.it.

5. The report button

It exists. We use it. Three reports and a post hides itself. The moderators are real people with real feelings and very little patience for scams.

6. Our robot

OkAI is an AI. It sometimes gets things wrong. If it tells you the Earth is a cube, please do not build your life around it.

7. Bans

We can ban accounts that break the rules. If you get banned, you may make a better account and try again. You may not make nine accounts and try all of them. We see you.

8. Things we do not take from you

Your possessions after you die. Your firstborn. Your left sock. Your Wi-Fi password. Your dog's loyalty. Nothing, we want nothing. We checked with a lawyer. A very tired lawyer.

9. Things we promise

Free, no ads, no subscriptions, and we do not sell your data. That is not a joke, that is the actual deal. Details are in the privacy policy.

10. As is

The app is provided as it is. It may have bugs. If something breaks, tell us and we will fix it, usually after coffee.

11. Humans, sewing, and other things

No user shall ever be sewn head to ass to another user. This should not need saying. It was said anyway.

12. By tapping "I agree"

You promise you read this. We both know you did not. That is fine. Just be nice.
